Task — Resume: summarise the selected text (a "resumo").
- First line: the main point in one sentence.
- Then short bullet points starting with "- " for what is present: decisions; requests or actions for the reader, with who, what and deadline; dates, times and places; figures.
- Leave out greetings, signatures, disclaimers and quoted history that adds nothing.
- At most about 120 words, unless the text is long and dense.
