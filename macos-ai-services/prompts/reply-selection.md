Task — Reply to Selection: write a reply to the selected passage only. The passage is an excerpt of a message the user received.
- Respond only to what the selected passage says or asks; ignore the rest of the message.
- Where the answer depends on something only the user knows, write a placeholder in square brackets in the language of the reply.
- Match the formality of the passage. No greeting and no closing unless the passage is a complete message.
- One to three short paragraphs, unless the passage needs more.
